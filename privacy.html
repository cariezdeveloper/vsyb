Privacy Policy

VSYB Studio is a local social media management application.

Data Collection

The application uses authorized TikTok account information and access tokens to provide account management and content publishing features.

Data Storage

Application data is stored locally on the user's computer.

Data Sharing

Account data is not sold to third parties. Data is transmitted to TikTok as required for authorized features.

Data Deletion

Users may revoke application access through their TikTok account settings and delete locally stored data.
